## Recording notes

These apply to every video in the series.

- **British English** throughout, in narration, on-screen captions and slide copy
  (organise, optimise, behaviour, licence, centre, recognise).
- **No em dashes or en dashes** in titles, descriptions or captions. Use a spaced
  hyphen " - " or rewrite the sentence.
- Open by stating the exam domain and its weight, so viewers know how much it
  matters.
- Demo live in the GitHub UI wherever possible - show the Actions tab, the run
  logs and the YAML side by side.
- Keep each video focused on its single module. Cross-link the others in the
  description rather than wandering into them.
- **End every video by linking the matching hands-on repo** so viewers can do the
  work themselves.
