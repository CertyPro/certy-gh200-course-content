# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want learning GitHub Actions to be welcoming for everyone, whatever your
experience, background or identity. Certy is a friendly place to study, ask
questions and improve, and we expect everyone who takes part to help keep it that
way.

## Our standards

Things that help our community:

- Being kind, patient and welcoming, especially to beginners.
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for fellow learners.

Things that are not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults or discriminatory comments of any kind.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional learning setting.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces - issues, pull requests,
discussions - and whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

If you see or experience unacceptable behaviour, please report it to the
maintainers at **contact@codedvisiondesign.co.uk**. Reports are handled promptly,
fairly and in confidence. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or
contributions that breach this code, and may suspend or ban anyone for repeated or
serious violations.
